# Volatile platform facts

Upload limits, aspect-ratio recommendations, monetization programs, APIs, replay retention, music rules, disclosure interfaces, and feature availability can change by platform, account, region, or app version.

Keep volatile values out of hard-coded workflow logic. Before delivery or publication:

1. Open the current official destination documentation.
2. Record the URL, observed requirement, account/region scope, checker, and time in `templates/platform-recheck-log.csv`.
3. Update a project-specific destination profile.
4. Render and inspect against the current interface.

Community tutorials can explain technique, but they should not override current official requirements.
