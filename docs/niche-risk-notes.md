# Niche risk notes

Evaluate niches by source access, moment supply, audience specificity, brand safety, differentiation, and a plausible value model. Add niche-specific checks for minors, medical/financial/legal claims, sports footage, game publisher rules, music, harassment, gambling, graphic material, private individuals, and misleading context.

Do not infer that a visible page owns its sources, is profitable, or has transferable results. Record confirmed facts, self-reports, opinions, and inferences separately.
