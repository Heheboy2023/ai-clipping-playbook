# Case-study evidence rules

- Give every claim a source ID and exact scope.
- Label earnings, views, client counts, and outcomes as self-reported unless records or independent evidence confirm them.
- Separate publication date, event period, and access date.
- Record selection bias, survivorship bias, attribution limits, sponsorship, and missing baselines.
- Do not treat a course sales page, tool testimonial, or viral post as independent validation.
- Explain what cannot transfer: access, audience, timing, talent, budget, team, rights, or platform conditions.
- Never copy a competitor’s title, language, course structure, or examples.
