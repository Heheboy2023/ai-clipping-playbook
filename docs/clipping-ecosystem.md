# Clipping ecosystem map

| Path | Asset owner | Primary work | Typical buyer/value | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creator/brand team | Creator or employer | Repurpose owned source | Awareness, education, conversion | Measuring attribution honestly |
| Freelance service | Client | Selection, editing, delivery | Reliable output and judgment | Scope, rights, revisions, access |
| Agency | Client portfolio | Team workflow and reporting | Capacity and consistency | QA, handoffs, margin, permissions |
| Owned page | Operator or licensors | Editorial programming | Audience asset | Source rights and platform dependence |
| Performance campaign | Varies | Incentivized distribution | Defined tracked outcome | Claim quality, attribution, terms |

The templates organize decisions and evidence. They do not guarantee demand, revenue, views, or legal compliance.
