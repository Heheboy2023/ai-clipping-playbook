# Supporting visual ideas

```text
Verified clip transcript with timestamps: [TRANSCRIPT]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Available assets: [ASSET LIST]

Suggest up to 5 supporting visuals. For each, give:
- timestamp;
- what should appear;
- why it helps;
- how long it should stay;
- whether the speaker's face should remain visible.

Use only the listed assets. Do not invent a photo, screenshot, event, person, product, or result.
Skip any moment that works better with the speaker on screen.
```
