# Title and description options

```text
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Platform: [PLATFORM]
Verified clip transcript: [TRANSCRIPT]
Main point: [MAIN POINT]
Tone: [TONE]

Write 10 short title options and 3 plain descriptions.
Keep the titles specific. Do not invent facts or promise a result the clip does not show.
For each title, identify the exact transcript line or idea that supports it.
```

Choose one option, then rewrite it in your own voice.
