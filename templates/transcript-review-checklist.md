# Transcript review checklist

- [ ] Media and transcript durations are plausibly aligned.
- [ ] Every segment has a start, end, text, and stable ID.
- [ ] Names, brands, figures, and technical terms were checked against audio.
- [ ] Speaker labels were reconciled by a human.
- [ ] Overlap, crosstalk, music, and inaudible sections are marked.
- [ ] Candidate boundaries were checked in the original media.
- [ ] Meaning, uncertainty, and negation were preserved.
- [ ] Any model-generated wording is identified and removed from verbatim quotes.
