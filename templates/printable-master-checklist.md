# Master clipping checklist

## Permission and intake

- [ ] Source owner/authority and intended use are recorded.
- [ ] Music, guest, likeness, sponsor, privacy, and destination limits are reviewed separately.
- [ ] Original is preserved; working copy is hashed and registered with a stable source ID.

## Editorial

- [ ] Audience, page promise, and clip job are stated.
- [ ] Candidate is checked against the source, not transcript alone.
- [ ] In/out points preserve context; hook and payoff are honest.
- [ ] Sensitive facts, names, quotations, and speaker identity receive human review.

## Edit, captions, and audio

- [ ] Crop/layout protects the active subject and important interface/gameplay information.
- [ ] Captions match speech, names, timing, line breaks, safe zones, and contrast.
- [ ] Audio has no new clipping, silence, missing channel, or distracting level jump.
- [ ] Full clip is watched at normal speed with sound.

## Render and package

- [ ] Expected container, streams, duration, dimensions, and audio presence pass automated QC.
- [ ] Final file and package use stable, portable names and recorded hashes.
- [ ] Destination requirements are rechecked from current official material.
- [ ] Rights, disclosure, music, metadata, and human approval records are complete.
- [ ] Packaging approval is not mistaken for publishing authority.

## Delivery and release

- [ ] Correct version is delivered through the agreed secure method.
- [ ] Live publication, if separately authorized, is verified in the destination.
- [ ] Corrections, archive, retention, and deletion follow the recorded plan.
