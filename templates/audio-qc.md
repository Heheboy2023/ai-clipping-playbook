# Audio QC

- [ ] Dialogue is intelligible on headphones and a phone speaker.
- [ ] No clipping, pumping, phase damage, or abrupt edits are audible.
- [ ] Loudness processing did not bury music or ambience.
- [ ] Music and third-party audio are authorized.
- [ ] Start and end transitions sound intentional.
- [ ] The normalized result was compared with the source.
- [ ] Human listening is recorded in the run notes.
