# Outreach scripts

## Ask to send a private sample

> yo, I've been watching your [PODCAST/STREAM]. I cut a short private sample from [MOMENT] because [SPECIFIC REASON]. I tightened it, reframed it for vertical, and added captions. Want me to send it?

## Send the sample

> here it is: [PRIVATE LINK]. I focused on [ONE EDITING CHOICE]. If you like the direction, I can price a small pack from the full episode.

## Follow up

> quick follow-up on the private sample from [EPISODE]. No pressure if the timing is off. If short clips are a priority, I can send a simple 3-clip option.

## Not now

> all good. I'll keep the sample private. If clips become a priority later, feel free to reach out.

## Referral

> glad the clips worked. Do you know one creator who could use the same kind of help? An intro would mean a lot.

Personalize every message. Do not mass-send the bracketed version.
