# Thirty-day income action plan

## Week 1 — Skill and offer

- Finish 3–5 focused clips.
- Choose one creator type.
- Write one simple offer.
- Set one test price.

## Week 2 — Prospect and sample

- Build a 25-person prospect list.
- Make 2 strong private samples.
- Send 10 personal messages.

## Week 3 — Calls and delivery

- Follow up.
- Run discovery calls.
- Quote a small package.
- Deliver any won work cleanly.

## Week 4 — Improve and repeat

- Review time and revisions.
- Ask for repeat work or a referral.
- Improve the portfolio.
- Send the next 10 personal messages.
