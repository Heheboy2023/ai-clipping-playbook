## Optional calculation

For a clearly labeled worksheet rate, add actions across the selected posts,
add their views, then divide total actions by total views and multiply by 1,000.
Do not divide by zero. Do not call views unique viewers. Keep raw counts,
platform definitions, post age, and missing values beside any calculated rate.
