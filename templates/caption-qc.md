# Caption QC

- [ ] Text matches the spoken meaning.
- [ ] Proper nouns and numbers are verified.
- [ ] Reading speed and line breaks are comfortable.
- [ ] Captions remain inside the destination safe area.
- [ ] Captions do not cover a face, product, score, or required disclosure.
- [ ] Music and sound labels are useful rather than decorative.
- [ ] The entire rendered file received human playback.
