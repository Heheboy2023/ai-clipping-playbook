# Synthetic analytics exercise

Populate `templates/clip-experiment-log.csv` with invented, clearly labeled practice values. Use it to learn definitions and confounders only; never cite the synthetic values as platform or earnings evidence.

`practice-results.csv` contains six entirely fictional posts for Chapter 25.
They share an invented account context, 30-second length, and 72-hour observation window. They are not a platform export or measured experiment. The two reaction rows have higher views but lower watch time and fewer follows than the answer rows. That supports a next-test choice inside the exercise, not a claim that answers outperform reactions generally.

Average watch seconds divided by length is an average fraction watched for this toy comparison, not the share of people who finished. Real dashboards use platform-specific denominators; retain the displayed metric name and definition.
