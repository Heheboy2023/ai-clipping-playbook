# Record three portfolio practice pieces

These exercises match Chapter 3 of *Clip It & Cash In*. They contain original fictional dialogue. Label the exports as practice, not client work. The tiny videos in `examples/fixtures/` are patterns and tones for code tests, not spoken or gameplay portfolio material.

## 1. Story

Record the script in [first-clip](../first-clip/README.md). Cut from “I spent an hour” through “get to the reason someone clicked.” Add your own caption times after editing. Recording speed determines the times.

Export: `portfolio-01-podcast-story.mp4`. Label: original story-edit exercise.

## 2. Reaction

Use your own gameplay, a creator-supplied recording, or this desk exercise:

1. Keep your face, a cup, and your hand visible in one wide phone shot.
2. Say, “Three tries. Let's see if I can get one.”
3. Try to drop a small paper ball into the cup from a short distance.
4. Record all three attempts continuously and react to what actually happens.
5. End with a truthful line such as “Got one” or “Okay, I need more practice.”

Keep each attempt connected to its own outcome. Cut long resets, not the action needed to understand the result. Crop only when the cup and face remain clear; pad the wide picture if needed.

Export: `portfolio-02-desk-reaction.mp4`. Label: original reaction-edit exercise. Use `portfolio-02-gaming-reaction.mp4` only if the source actually contains gameplay.

## 3. Two-speaker conversation

Record with a friend, or read both roles from different sides of the same frame. A solo version is a staged role-play.

**Host:** What's one thing you would change about your first editing job?

**Editor:** I'd ask how much source footage I had to watch. I thought I was editing three short clips. Then I opened a two-hour recording with no timestamps.

**Host:** So the hard part wasn't only the cutting?

**Editor:** Right. Finding the moments was a separate job. Now I ask for the source length before I give a price.

Keep the question, problem, and answer. Switch framing with the speaker. Add captions after the cut. This is invented teaching dialogue, not your client history.

Export: `portfolio-03-two-speaker.mp4`. Label: staged two-role editing exercise, or original two-person practice recording as appropriate.

Use `templates/mini-portfolio.md`. Give each clip a name, source type, moment type, short edit note, and practice/client status. Check the actual exports on a phone. Keep rough versions outside the viewing folder.
